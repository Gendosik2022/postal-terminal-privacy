Postal Terminal Privacy Policy

Effective: August 8 2026

1. Developer and contact

Developer: Gennady Kuryukov. Privacy and support contact: g.kuriukov@yandex.ru.

2. Game data

Postal Terminal does not create accounts or request your name, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. Game progress, level results, achievements, sound settings, and your advertising choice are stored locally on the device. You can erase them by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising and your choice

The Android and iOS versions use Yandex Mobile Ads only for full-screen interstitial advertising. The first ad may appear after level 10 and then after every tenth completed level. The advertising SDK is not initialized and does not load ads before you choose a mode under Advertising:

Consent • personalized sends Yandex consent to process advertising data. Ads may use the advertising identifier and inferred interests.

Decline • non-personalized sends Yandex a refusal of consent. The game requests restricted non-personalized ads; where legally permitted, minimal technical data may still be processed to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, secure the service, and prevent fraud.

Declining does not restrict the game. You can change your choice at any time on the main screen. Precise location and app gameplay-event reporting for advertising analytics are disabled by the developer.

4. Data the advertising service may process

Depending on country, device settings, and selected mode, Yandex may process the IP address, device and app information, language, approximate network region, an advertising identifier when consent and system permission are available, and ad request, impression, and interaction events. Yandex processes this data under its privacy policy. The developer requires the advertising partner to provide protection no lower than applicable store and legal requirements.

5. Legal bases and countries

Advertising settings are available regardless of country. Yandex's setUserConsent method receives the effective choice before SDK initialization and on every app launch. On iOS, personalized mode additionally requires separate App Tracking Transparency permission. If permission is denied or restricted, the game sends refusal to Yandex, requests non-personalized advertising, and does not use IDFA or cross-app tracking.

6. Sharing, retention, and children

The developer does not sell locally stored game progress. Clear app data or uninstall the game to delete local data. Yandex determines retention and deletion rules for advertising data. To withdraw advertising consent, choose non-personalized mode on the main screen. To request deletion or ask about advertising-data processing, contact the developer through the support page; when necessary, the request will be forwarded to the advertising partner. The game has no chat, user content, or personal-data entry. If a child uses the app, a parent or legal guardian should make the advertising choice as required by local law.

7. Policy changes

If SDKs or data processing change, this page and the app-store disclosures will be updated before the related release.

8. Support

Instructions and contacts are on the support page. Developer site: gendosik2022.github.io.
