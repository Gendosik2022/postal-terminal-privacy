Postal Terminal Privacy Policy

Effective: August 8 2026

1. Developer and contact

Developer: Gennady Kuryukov. Privacy and support contact: g.kuriukov@yandex.ru.

2. Game data

Postal Terminal does not create accounts or request your name, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. Game progress, level results, achievements, sound settings, and your advertising choice are stored locally on the device. You can erase them by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising and your choice

The game uses Yandex Mobile Ads only for full-screen interstitial advertising. An ad may appear after every tenth completed level. On the main screen under Advertising you choose one mode. The advertising SDK does not load ads before you make a choice:

Personalized ads may use the advertising identifier and inferred interests. On iOS this mode is used only after Apple system tracking permission.

Non-personalized ads are not selected using your interests. Limited technical data may still be processed to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, secure the service, and prevent fraud.

Declining personalization does not restrict the game. Precise location and app gameplay-event reporting for advertising analytics are disabled by the developer.

4. Data the advertising service may process

Depending on the platform, country, device settings, and selected mode, Yandex may process the IP address, device and app information, language, approximate network region, an advertising identifier where permitted, and ad request, impression, and interaction events. Yandex processes this data under its privacy policy.

5. Legal bases and countries

Advertising settings are available regardless of country. Where consent is legally required, the advertising SDK receives your selected state before ads are loaded. You can change the choice at any time on the main screen. Personalized advertising on Apple devices also follows the App Tracking Transparency system permission.

6. Sharing, retention, and children

The developer does not sell locally stored game progress. Yandex determines retention periods for its advertising data. The game has no chat, user content, or personal-data entry. If a child uses the app, a parent or legal guardian should make the advertising choice as required by local law.

7. Policy changes

If SDKs or data processing change, this page and the app-store disclosures will be updated before the related release.

8. Support

Instructions and contacts are on the support page. Developer site: gendosik2022.github.io.
